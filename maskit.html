Maskit is a speculative brand and immersive desert retreat that explores memory, cultural preservation, and storytelling through symbolic experiences, artifacts, and rituals inspired by the Negev. The project combines branding, spatial design, printed matter, and interactive elements to create a place where memories are collected, protected, and carried into the future.

We began developing a visual language that guests could gradually discover, creating a sense of belonging through shared symbols.

​

We deconstructed Maskit’s original logo into five shapes, then recombined them into a new symbolic language.

SECRET LANGUAGE

The project began with a speculative constraint: a brand that exists entirely offline. We reimagined how Maskit could communicate through physical encounters, objects, and spaces.

THE BRIEF

We imagined a retreat in the Negev, removed from the pace of city life and constant digital connection. The desert offered a setting for slowing down, creating space for memory, ritual, and human connection.
