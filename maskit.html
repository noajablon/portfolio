01/ Maskit
FIELDBrandingYEAR2026PLACEBezalel

Maskit is a speculative brand and immersive desert retreat that explores memory, cultural preservation, and storytelling through symbolic experiences, artifacts, and rituals inspired by the Negev. The project combines branding, spatial design, printed matter.

1.1
1.2

The brief

The project began with a speculative constraint: a brand that exists entirely offline. We reimagined how Maskit could communicate through physical encounters, objects, and spaces.

1.3

Secret language

We began developing a visual language that guests could gradually discover, creating a sense of belonging through shared symbols. We deconstructed Maskit’s original logo into five shapes, then recombined them into a new symbolic language.

1.4
1.5

Geometric patterns

The symbolic language was further developed into intricate, embroidery-inspired patterns, each composed of the letters spelling its symbol’s name.

The bird

Represents migration and continuity, carrying memories and traditions across places and generations.

The flower

Symbolizes renewal, expressing how cultural roots can flourish in new surroundings.

The eye

Represents awareness and remembrance, inviting us to observe, reflect, and preserve the stories around us.

1.6

The Negev

We imagined a retreat in the Negev, removed from the pace of city life and constant digital connection. The desert offered a setting for slowing down, creating space for memory, ritual, and human connection.
