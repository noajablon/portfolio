THE BRIEF
The brief was to create an explainer video about a topic connected to Jerusalem. I chose to investigate the strange occurrences in my own apartment in Nachlaot, turning a personal experience into the starting point for the film.
THE TECHNIQUE
I developed a visual language rooted in my own illustration style, combining AI tools with animation in After Effects. Bringing these techniques together helped me translate my personal experiences into motion and shape the atmosphere of the film.
