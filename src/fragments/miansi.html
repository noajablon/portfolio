09/ MIANSI

Miansi is a silk hair accessory brand concept developed as a group project during my semester abroad in China. Together, we explored how natural Chinese silk could bring cultural traditions into everyday hair care and styling.

THE PROJECT

We drew inspiration from hairstyles and beauty traditions across different cultures, reinterpreting them through contemporary accessories. Chinese silk became the shared material connecting these influences within one brand.

ACROSS CULTURES

The packaging carries the floral patterns into the product experience, giving each collection its own identity within the Miansi brand. It connects the silk accessories to the cultural inspiration behind their design.

THE PACKAGING

Flowers associated with each country inspired a series of vector patterns. Each pattern gave its collection a distinct cultural reference while maintaining a consistent graphic language across the brand.
