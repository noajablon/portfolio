WORK WITH ME

BRANDING

The brief was to create an event around a historic site in Jerusalem. I developed a running event in Lifta, using the location as the starting point for its visual identity.

THE BRIEF

I was drawn to Lifta’s political weight, its beauty, and the special energy surrounding it. These qualities inspired an event with a sense of spirituality, curiosity, and mystery.

THE STARTING POINT

I visited Lifta to photograph the site, building the project around what I captured. My photographs became the foundation of the visual language, grounding the identity in a personal encounter with the place.
