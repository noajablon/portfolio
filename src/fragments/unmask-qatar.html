Unmask Qatar is a freelance visual identity project for an initiative seeking to build a movement across Israel. The brief called for a bold, provocative identity that would urge people to “wake up,” join the initiative, and amplify its message.

THE BRIEF

Across posters, Instagram posts, and a reel, I developed a confrontational visual language designed to interrupt everyday scrolling and demand attention.

​

Clear information and direct calls to action encouraged viewers to follow, join, and make noise around the campaign.
