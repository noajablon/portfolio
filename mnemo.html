The brief was to design an AI-based platform.

We approached it through speculative design, exploring a future in which technology allows personal memories to remain accessible beyond a person’s lifetime.

THE BRIEF

Mnemo emerged in response to October 7 and the many families facing the loss of loved ones.

​

The project explores how a digital archive might preserve a sense of connection, allowing stories and shared moments to be revisited over time.

THE STARTING POINT

The concept begins with consent: during their lifetime, a person grants the platform access to selected texts, images, and videos, which AI helps organize into a memory archive.

​

Authorized family and friends can contribute their own memories, with access restricted to invited participants.

BUILDING THE ARCHIVE

We chose a nostalgic, lighthearted visual direction to make space for warmth within remembrance. The interface encourages visitors to connect with the person’s life, personality, and everyday moments.
