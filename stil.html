THE PROJECT

Biofeedback is a mind-body technique that measures involuntary bodily functions such as heart rate, muscle tension, and skin temperature.

It translates this data into real-time visual or auditory cues, teaching you to consciously regulate these responses and manage stress, pain, or anxiety

BIOFEEDBACK

Through computer vision and remote pulse sensing (rPPG), Stil is designed to recognize signs of stress and adapt its sensory environment, including sound and acoustic isolation.
